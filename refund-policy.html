Return and Refund Policy.

How payments, cancellations and refunds work at Interlinkz, in simple words.
Last updated: November 2026

Our approach

We want every client to know where they stand before work starts. This page explains how payments, cancellations and refunds work at Interlinkz, in plain words.

Before work starts

If you have paid for a project and no work has started yet, tell us in writing and we will refund the full payment for that project.

After work has started

Once design or build work has started, your payment covers the work done up to that point. If you stop the project, we will share the work completed so far, and the part of the payment that covers work already done is not refundable. Anything you paid beyond the work done is returned to you.

Designs and approval

We show you the design before we build anything. If the first design direction is not right, we revise it within the same project scope. A change of mind after a design has been approved and built may be charged as new work.

Completed and delivered work

When a website or design is delivered and approved by you, the payment for that work is final.

Monthly services

For monthly services such as marketing or support, you can stop the service for the next month at any time. The current month, once it has started, is not refundable.

How to ask for a refund

Write to contact@interlinkz.com with your project name and the reason. We reply by email, confirm the amount and explain the next steps.

Questions about this page? Email us at contact@interlinkz.com.
